📡 How to connect via Bluetooth
  1. Pair your phone with your Windows PC in Bluetooth Settings.
  2. On your phone, turn on Bluetooth Tethering (Settings → Hotspot/Tethering → Bluetooth Tethering).
  3. On PC, right-click the Bluetooth icon in system tray → Join a Personal Area Network → Right click phone → Connect using Access Point.
  4. Open this page on your phone browser and tap Start Audio!
  5. (Or simply stay on the same Wi-Fi network for instant connection!)
